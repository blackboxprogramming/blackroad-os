# 08. RoadBook

**RoadBook** is digital-native publishing.

It is for books, audiobooks, research, essays, notes, text media, peer review, provenance, comments, authorship, narration, royalties, and collaboration.

It exists because people want to write, read, narrate, review, sell, and preserve text/audio work without being trapped by old publishing gates or terrible creator economics.

RoadBook should support the full lifecycle of written and spoken work with proper versioning, rights management, and RoadChain provenance.

**Primary integrations:**
- RoadOS for the writing/reading surface
- BlackBoard for visual companions
- RoadView for discovery
- RoadChain for provenance and royalties tracking
- RoadWork when publishing becomes business

**Core promise:**
Write, publish, and preserve without losing ownership or context.

**Status:** Canon-ready (Level 4). Implementation planned.
