# 17. RoadBand

**RoadBand** is music creation, hosting, taste profiling, recommendation, production, and car-first listening.

It should work for people who know what sounds good even if they cannot operate a DAW. The “ear doctor” idea matters: let users compare sounds, choose what feels right, build a taste profile, generate or guide production, host songs, unify Spotify/Apple/YouTube/SoundCloud-like sources, and recommend music better than current platforms.

**Primary integrations:**
- RoadOS for listening/creation surface
- BlackBoard for visual music assets
- RoadShow for music programming
- RoadChain for rights and provenance
- RoadWork when music becomes business

**Core promise:**
Create, discover, and enjoy music with taste intelligence and ownership preserved.

**Status:** Canon-ready (Level 4). Implementation planned.
