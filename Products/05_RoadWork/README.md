# 05. RoadWork

**RoadWork** is the “anything involving work” product.

RoadWork is where business, employment, operations, compliance, filings, taxes, registrations, trademarks, Stripe/Atlas, state registrations, backend business setup, internal procedures, task execution, and company workflows live.

RoadWork is not only for corporations. It is for anyone trying to do work, start work, organize work, operate a business, run a team, complete required steps, or understand what the next obligation is.

RoadWork should answer:
- What do I need to do?
- What order do I do it in?
- What portal or document is involved?
- What proof do I need?
- What deadline matters?
- What does this mean legally/operationally?
- What should be recorded?
- What should route to RoadWire, RoadMap, or RoadChain?

RoadWork should include:
- business formation steps
- Stripe/Atlas style startup workflows
- state/federal registration tracking
- trademarks/IP tasks
- tax/compliance reminders
- backend operational setup
- SOPs
- vendor/customer workflows
- campaign execution if social/media becomes business work
- reports/dashboards handoff to RoadMap

RoadWork must not:
- become generic project management
- make legal/tax claims without uncertainty and source checking
- hide required human approval
- confuse live collaboration with CarPool
- confuse communication records with RoadWire

**Primary integrations:**
- RoadMap for status/reporting
- RoadWire for formal/non-immediate messages
- RoadChain for proof/receipts
- CarKeys for authority/access
- OneWay for data ownership flows
- RoadCode for backend/internal systems
- BackRoad when social becomes business execution

**Core promise:**
“This is the work. These are the steps. Here is the proof.”

**Status:** Canon-ready (Level 4). Implementation planned.
