# 06. BackRoad

**BackRoad** is BlackRoad’s social layer.

BackRoad has two views:

1. The all-social feed view: A unified feed that can bring together the user’s social media from external platforms and BackRoad itself.
2. The native BackRoad social view: BlackRoad’s own creator/community social app.

BackRoad exists because modern social platforms became algorithmic, ad-heavy, manipulative, and creator-hostile. BackRoad should help users and creators regain ownership, context, and sanity.

BackRoad should support:
- social feed aggregation
- native BackRoad posts
- creator/community profiles
- comments and replies
- creator ownership
- social graph portability
- community discovery
- consent-based personalization
- routing social work into RoadWork when it becomes marketing/campaign execution

BackRoad must not:
- become another algorithmic addiction farm
- hide why users see posts
- turn creators into ad inventory
- confuse social browsing with business analytics
- own the user’s social graph in a trapped way

**Routing rule:** If the user is viewing, posting, following, or engaging socially, use BackRoad. If the user is planning campaigns, measuring marketing performance, doing business reporting, or managing creator operations, route to RoadWork.

**Primary integrations:** RoadWire for long-term community/meetings.

**Status:** Canon-ready (Level 4). Implementation planned.
